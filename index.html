What we do

From one extra socket to a full rewire, for homes, rentals and businesses. Choose a service to see what's included.

Rewires & new circuits

Full and partial rewires for older properties, and new circuits for extensions, kitchens, garages and outbuildings. Planned around how you use each room.

What's included

  • Full and partial rewires
  • New circuits for extensions and renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom wiring
  • Garages, sheds and outbuildings
Ask about rewires & new circuits

Fuse board upgrades

Old fuse boards replaced with a modern consumer unit and RCD protection. Every circuit is labelled and tested before the power goes back on.

What's included

  • Consumer unit replacements
  • RCD and RCBO protection
  • Clearly labelled circuits
  • Full test on completion
Ask about fuse board upgrades

Lighting

Indoor and outdoor lighting, from replacing one fitting to lighting a whole kitchen or garden.

What's included

  • Downlights and pendant fittings
  • Kitchen and under-cabinet lighting
  • Dimmers and two-way switching
  • Garden and security lighting
Ask about lighting

Sockets & switches

Extra sockets where you need them, USB and outdoor sockets, and switches moved or replaced.

What's included

  • Extra or moved sockets
  • USB and outdoor sockets
  • Switches and fused spurs
  • Cooker and appliance connections
Ask about sockets & switches

Fault finding & repairs

Flickering lights, dead sockets or a trip switch that won't stay up. We trace the fault, explain what's wrong and put it right safely.

What's included

  • Tripping RCDs and breakers
  • Dead sockets and circuits
  • Flickering or failed lighting
  • Damaged and scorched fittings
Ask about fault finding & repairs

Testing & inspection

Inspection and testing for homeowners, landlords, buyers and businesses, showing the condition of the installation and anything that needs attention.

What's included

  • Periodic inspection and testing (EICR)
  • Checks for landlords and rentals
  • Checks before buying a property
  • Shops, offices and workshops
